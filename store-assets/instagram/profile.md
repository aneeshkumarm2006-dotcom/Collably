## 3. The rest of the profile

- **Account type:** Professional → Creator? No. Choose **Business**, category
  **App Page** (or Marketing Agency). Business unlocks the contact button and
  the full insights tab.
- **Contact:** hello@localcreatorcrew.com, and set the city as Toronto (or
  whichever city you launch in). A real address makes the local angle credible.
- **Link:** add up to 5 links, in this order
  1. Get the app (Play Store link)
  2. Post a collab (business signup)
  3. www.localcreatorcrew.com
- **Story highlights (5):** How it works · For Creators · For Brands ·
  Open Collabs · FAQ. Covers = white pin on the brand blue, one icon each.

## 5. Content pillars, weekly

| Pillar | Frequency | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Open collabs this week | 2x | Your live campaign list IS content. No competitor can copy it. |
| Creator spotlight | 1x | Creators repost. Free reach into exactly your audience. |
| Local business feature | 1x | Businesses repost, and it recruits the supply side. |
| Educate (rates, pitching, disclosure rules) | 1x | Saves and shares, which is what the ranking actually rewards. |

Geotag every single post to the city and neighbourhood. For a location-based
product this is the highest-leverage free distribution you have.
