## Full description (4000 char max)

```
Local Creator Crew connects local businesses with nearby creators for gifting campaigns and collaborations. Whether you run a café, salon, boutique, or studio — or you're a creator who loves sharing local finds — this is where you find each other.

FOR CREATORS
• Discover collab opportunities from businesses near you
• See how far away each campaign is before you apply
• Apply with a quick pitch and your profile
• Chat directly with a business once you're matched
• Submit your content and get it verified, all in one place
• Verify your Instagram so businesses know you're the real deal

FOR BUSINESSES
• Post a campaign in minutes and reach local creators
• Review applications with creator profiles and pitches
• Message matched creators directly in the app
• Track submissions and verify completed collabs
• Build relationships with creators in your community

WHY LOCAL CREATOR CREW
• Built around your neighbourhood — collabs are matched by location
• Simple, focused, and made for real local partnerships
• No spam: creators and businesses verify their identity
• Safety tools built in — report and block anytime

HOW IT WORKS
1. Sign up as a creator or a business
2. Creators browse nearby campaigns; businesses post their own
3. Apply or review applications
4. Get matched and chat
5. Create, submit, and verify the collab

Local Creator Crew is free to join. Start finding your next local collaboration today.

Questions? Reach us at hello@localcreatorcrew.com
```
